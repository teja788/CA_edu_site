## Section 4, Indian Partnership Act 1932 — definition of partnership

- **Bare-act line relied on:** "'Partnership' is the relation between persons who
  have agreed to share the profits of a business carried on by all or any of them
  acting for all. Persons who have entered into partnership with one another are
  called individually 'partners' and collectively 'a firm', and the name under
  which their business is carried on is called the 'firm name'."

## Section 6 and its Explanation — mode of determining existence of partnership

- **Bare-act line relied on:** "In determining whether a group of persons is or is
  not a firm, or whether a person is or is not a partner in a firm, regard shall
  be had to the real relation between the parties, as shown by all relevant facts
  taken together." The Explanations then provide that the sharing of profits or
  of gross returns arising from property by persons holding a joint or common
  interest in that property does not of itself make such persons partners, and
  that the receipt by a person of a share of the profits of a business, or of a
  payment contingent upon the earning of profits or varying with the profits
  earned by a business, does not of itself make him a partner — in particular in
  the case of a lender, a servant or agent remunerated by a share of profits, the
  widow or child of a deceased partner receiving an annuity, and a previous owner
  or part owner of the business receiving a share of profits in consideration of
  the sale of the goodwill.
- **Source:** India Code, Act 9 of 1932, s. 6 — transcribed 7 Aug 2026. The exact
  numbering of the two Explanations, and whether the five illustrations sit in
  Explanation II, need checking.
- **Used in:** §3 (whole section, including the table of five non-partners), §22
  vignette 1; q-f2c4-004, cs-f2c4-01-a, cs-f2c4-01-b, d-f2c4-01.

## Sections 9 and 10 — general duties; duty to indemnify for fraud

## Sections 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23 and 24 — the firm and third parties

- **Bare-act line relied on:** s. 18 — "Subject to the provisions of this Act, a

## Section 28 — holding out

- **Bare-act line relied on:** s. 28(1) — "Anyone who by words spoken or written
  or by conduct represents himself, or knowingly permits himself to be

## Section 29 — rights of a transferee of a partner's interest

- **Bare-act line relied on:** a transfer by a partner of his interest, absolute
  or by mortgage or charge, does not entitle the transferee, during the
  continuance of the firm, to interfere in the conduct of the business, to require
  accounts, or to inspect the books of the firm, but entitles him only to receive
  the share of profits of the transferring partner, and the transferee is bound to
  accept the account of profits agreed to by the partners; on dissolution, or on
  the transferring partner ceasing to be a partner, the transferee is entitled to
  receive the share of the assets to which the transferring partner was entitled
  and, for the purpose of ascertaining that share, to an account as from the date
  of the dissolution.
- **Source:** India Code, Act 9 of 1932, s. 29 — transcribed 7 Aug 2026.
- **Used in:** §17, §4.1 (transfer of share row), the one-page summary;

## Sections 36 and 37 — the outgoing partner

- **Bare-act line relied on:** s. 36(1) — an outgoing partner may carry on a

  subject to contract to the contrary, may not use the firm name, represent

## Section 72 — mode of giving public notice

- **Bare-act line relied on:** a public notice under this Act is given, where it
  relates to the retirement or expulsion of a partner from a registered firm, or
  to the dissolution of a registered firm, or to the election to become or not to
  become a partner in a registered firm by a person attaining majority who was
  admitted as a minor to the benefits of partnership, by notice to the Registrar
  of Firms under s. 63 and by publication in the Official Gazette and in at least
  one vernacular newspaper circulating in the district where the firm to which it
  relates has its place or principal place of business; and in any other case, by
  publication in the Official Gazette and in at least one such vernacular
  newspaper.
- **Source:** India Code, Act 9 of 1932, s. 72 — transcribed 7 Aug 2026. Note the
  structure: the notice to the Registrar is required only for a **registered**
  firm, and the chapter says so, but the reviewer should confirm that the
  chapter's phrasing in §21 does not over-state the requirement for an

## Companies Act 2013, section 464 — maximum number of partners

- **Proposition relied on:** an association or partnership of more than the
  prescribed number of persons (not exceeding one hundred, the number currently
  prescribed by rule being **fifty**) formed for the purpose of carrying on a
