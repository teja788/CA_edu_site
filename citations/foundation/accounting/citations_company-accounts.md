## Section 43 explanation — meaning of preference share capital

- **Position relied on (paraphrase of the Explanation to s. 43, not quoted):**
  preference share capital is that part of the issued share capital which
  carries, or would carry, a preferential right with respect to payment of
  dividend (a fixed amount or a fixed rate) and with respect to repayment of
  the amount of share capital on a winding up or repayment of capital.

## Section 52, Companies Act 2013 — securities premium and its permitted uses

- **Bare-act line relied on:** "Where a company issues shares at a premium,
  whether for cash or otherwise, a sum equal to the aggregate amount of the
  premium received on those shares shall be transferred to a 'securities premium
  account' …"; and the account may be applied by the company — "(a) towards the
  issue of unissued shares of the company to the members of the company as fully
  paid bonus shares; (b) in writing off the preliminary expenses of the company;
  (c) in writing off the expenses of, or the commission paid or discount allowed
  on, any issue of shares or debentures of the company; (d) in providing for the
  premium payable on the redemption of any redeemable preference shares or of
  any debentures of the company; or (e) for the purchase of its own shares or
  other securities under section 68."
- **Source:** India Code, Companies Act 2013, s. 52 — transcribed 7 Aug 2026,
  not re-fetched. **Note for the reviewer:** s. 52(2) originally carried a
  proviso restricting certain classes of company to uses (a), (b) and (e); that
  proviso was omitted by the Companies (Amendment) Act 2017. Please confirm the
  current text before initialling.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-018 (the "not permitted" option is dividend),
  q-f1c11-020 (write-offs against the account), q-f1c11-034 and q-f1c11-035

## Section 39, Companies Act 2013 — minimum subscription and application money

- **Bare-act line relied on (s. 39(2)):** "The amount payable on application on
  every security shall not be less than five per cent. of the nominal amount of
  the security or such other percentage or amount, as may be specified by the
  Securities and Exchange Board by making regulations in this behalf."
- **Position relied on (paraphrase of s. 39(1) and (3)):** no allotment may be
  made unless the amount stated in the prospectus as the minimum subscription
  has been subscribed and the sums payable on application received; if that has
  not happened within thirty days of the issue of the prospectus (or such period
  as SEBI specifies), the amount received must be returned within the prescribed
  time and manner.
- **Source:** India Code, Companies Act 2013, s. 39 — transcribed 7 Aug 2026,
  not re-fetched. The SEBI (ICDR) Regulations requirement of 25% of the issue
  price for a public issue is stated in the notes as a comparison only and is
  **not** examined in any question.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-016 (the 5% floor), q-f1c11-017 (refund on failure of
  minimum subscription), notes §5.

## Table F, Schedule I, Companies Act 2013 — interest on arrears and advance

- **Position relied on (paraphrase of the model articles, Table F):** where a
  call remains unpaid after the day appointed for payment, the member shall pay
  interest from the appointed day to the day of actual payment **at a rate not
  exceeding ten per cent per annum**, which the directors may waive wholly or in
  part; and the directors may, on money paid in advance of calls, pay interest
  **at a rate not exceeding twelve per cent per annum** as may be agreed. Table F
  also provides that a call shall not exceed one-fourth of the nominal value of
  the share, that calls shall be at least a month apart, and that fourteen days'
  notice of a call shall be given.
- **Source:** India Code, Companies Act 2013, Schedule I, Table F (model
  articles for a company limited by shares) — transcribed 7 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched. **Reviewer note:** both figures are stated in this chapter as
  MAXIMUM rates, not fixed rates; the stems in q-f1c11-027 and q-f1c11-028 say
  "at the maximum rate Table F allows" for exactly that reason.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-027 (10% on arrears), q-f1c11-028 (12% on advance),
  q-f1c11-029 (interest payable even in a loss year), notes §5 (call limits),
  §8, worked example 4, one-page revision summary.

## Section 2(85), Companies Act 2013 (with the Rules) — small company

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** a small company is a company, other than
  a public company, whose paid-up share capital and turnover do not exceed the
  prescribed limits; and the definition expressly excludes a holding or
  subsidiary company, a company registered under section 8, and a company or
  body corporate governed by any special Act.

## ICAI SM convention — forfeiture of shares (par and premium variants)

- **Position relied on (paraphrase, not quoted):** on forfeiture, Share Capital
  is debited with the amount **called up** on the forfeited shares; the
  instalments called and unpaid are credited to close the relevant allotment and
  call accounts; the balance — the capital actually received — is credited to
  Share Forfeiture Account. Where the shares were issued at a premium, premium
  **already received** is not written back, while premium **called but not
  received** is debited back to Securities Premium Account. Confirmed present in
  the current ICAI SM Ch 11 Unit 2 (May 2026 onwards edition) in the same terms,
  7 Aug 2026.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-033, q-f1c11-034, q-f1c11-035, q-f1c11-036, q-f1c11-037,
  d-f1c11-04 (points 1–3), notes §10, worked example 3.

## ICAI SM convention — reissue of forfeited shares and capital reserve

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** forfeited shares may be reissued at par,
  at a premium or at a discount; the discount allowed on reissue may not exceed
  the amount forfeited on those very shares; any amount received above the paid-
  up value on reissue is credited to Securities Premium; and after reissue the
  amount forfeited on the shares reissued, less the discount allowed on their
  reissue, is transferred to Capital Reserve. Where only part of the forfeited
  holding is reissued, the forfeited amount is apportioned per share, and the
  balance continues to be shown as an addition to subscribed capital.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-038, q-f1c11-039, q-f1c11-040, q-f1c11-041, q-f1c11-042,
  d-f1c11-04 (points 4–6), notes §11, worked example 3.

## ICAI SM convention — shares issued for consideration other than cash

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** assets and liabilities taken over are
  recorded first and the vendor credited with the purchase consideration, which
  is then discharged by an issue of shares; the number of shares issued is the
  purchase consideration divided by the **issue price** (face value plus
  premium), with the premium element credited to Securities Premium.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-031, q-f1c11-032, notes §9.

## ICAI SM convention — issue of debentures, discount and loss on issue

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** Debentures Account is credited with the
  **face value** of the debentures however they are priced; a discount on issue
  is a capital loss written off over the life of the debentures (against
  securities premium where available, otherwise against the statement of profit
  and loss); where debentures are redeemable at a premium, the discount on issue
  and the premium payable on redemption are together debited to Loss on Issue of
  Debentures Account at the time of issue, the redemption premium being credited
  to Premium on Redemption of Debentures Account as a liability. A premium
  received on issue is credited to Securities Premium and is not netted against
  the loss.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-044, q-f1c11-045, q-f1c11-046, d-f1c11-06 (points 1–4),
  notes §12, §13, worked example 5.
- **Spot-checked by:** _(blank)_

## ICAI SM convention — debentures issued as collateral security

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** two presentations are accepted. Under the
  first, no entry is passed and the issue of debentures as collateral security
  is disclosed by way of a note under the loan. Under the second, Debenture
  Suspense Account is debited and Debentures Account credited with the nominal
  value, and the Debenture Suspense balance is shown as a deduction from the
  debentures so issued, leaving the balance sheet total unaffected.
- **Used in:** q-f1c11-048, d-f1c11-06 (points 5–6), notes §14, worked example 5
  (working note 4).

## ICAI SM convention — interest on debentures (and TDS)

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** debenture interest is calculated on the
  nominal (face) value at the coupon rate, is a charge against profit payable

  source, the deduction being credited to a TDS payable account.
