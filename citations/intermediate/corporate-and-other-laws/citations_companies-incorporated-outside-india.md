## Section 2(42), Companies Act 2013 — definition of a foreign company

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** "foreign company" means any company or
  body corporate incorporated outside India which **(a)** has a place of
  business in India, whether by itself or through an agent, physically or
  through electronic mode, **and (b)** conducts any business activity in India
  in any other manner. The notes treat these as **three cumulative limbs**
  (incorporation outside India · place of business in India · conduct of
  business activity in India) and state expressly that failing any one takes
  the company outside Chapter XXII.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 2(42) — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched. **Reviewer note:** confirm the conjunction between clauses (a) and
  (b) reads "and" in the notified text, since the whole three-limb analysis and

## Rule 2, Companies (Registration of Foreign Companies) Rules 2014 — electronic mode and place of business

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** "electronic mode" means carrying out
  electronically, **whether the main server is installed in India or not**,
  business-to-business and business-to-consumer transactions, data interchange
  and other digital supply transactions; offering to accept deposits or
  inviting deposits or accepting deposits or subscriptions in securities in
  India or from citizens of India; financial settlements, web-based marketing,
  advisory and transactional services, database services and products, supply
  chain management; online services such as telemarketing, telecommuting,
  telemedicine, education and information research; and all related data
  communication services, whether conducted by e-mail, mobile devices, social
  media, cloud computing, document management, voice or data transmission or
  otherwise. "Place of business" is defined in the Rules in the same inclusive
  terms as s. 386(c).

## Isolated transactions and the meaning of a settled presence

- **Position relied on (paraphrase of the SM's analysis, not quoted):** an
  **isolated transaction** concluded in India — a single export sale negotiated
  by correspondence, a one-off visit, a sale through an independent buyer acting
  on his own account — does not by itself amount to establishing a **place of
  business** in India; what the definition contemplates is a **settled,
  continuing presence** from which business is done, coupled with actual
  business activity.
- **Source:** ICAI SM P2 Ch 11 (May 2026 ed.) scope — transcribed from memory
  27 Aug 2026, restated in our own words; **no SM sentence reproduced**.

## Section 379, Companies Act 2013 — application of the Act to foreign companies

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **s. 379(1)** — **ss. 380 to 386 (both
  inclusive) and ss. 392 and 393 apply to all foreign companies**. **s. 379(2)**
  — where **not less than fifty per cent of the paid-up share capital**, whether
  **equity or preference or partly equity and partly preference**, of a company
  incorporated outside India and having a place of business in India is held by
  **one or more citizens of India** or by **one or more companies or bodies
  corporate incorporated in India**, or by **any combination of them**, whether
  **singly or in the aggregate**, such company shall comply with **the provisions
  of this Chapter** and **such other provisions of this Act as may be
  prescribed**, with regard to the **business carried on by it in India**, **as
  if it were a company incorporated in India**.

## Section 380, Companies Act 2013 — documents to be delivered to the Registrar

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** every foreign company which **establishes
  a place of business in India** shall, **within thirty days of the
  establishment**, deliver to the Registrar for registration — **(a)** a
  certified copy of the charter, statutes or memorandum and articles, or other
  instrument constituting or defining the constitution of the company, with a
  **certified translation in English** if the instrument is not in English;
  **(b)** the full address of the **registered or principal office** of the
  company; **(c)** a list of the **directors and secretary** with the prescribed
  particulars; **(d)** the **name and address of one or more persons resident in
  India** authorised to accept on behalf of the company **service of process**
  and any notices or other documents required to be served on the company;
  **(e)** the full address of the office of the company in India deemed to be its
  **principal place of business** in India; **(f)** particulars of the **opening
  and closing of a place of business in India on any earlier occasion or
  occasions**; **(g)** a **declaration** that none of the directors of the company
  or the authorised representative in India has ever been **convicted or debarred
  from formation of companies and management in India or abroad**; and **(h)**
  any other prescribed information. **s. 380(3)** — where any **alteration** is
  made or occurs in those particulars, a return containing the particulars of the
  alteration must be delivered to the Registrar **within thirty days of the
  alteration**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 380 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not

## Section 381, Companies Act 2013, and Rule 4 — accounts of a foreign company

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **381(1)** — every foreign company shall,
  in **every calendar year**, make out a **balance sheet and profit and loss
  account** in the prescribed form, containing the prescribed particulars and
  including or having annexed the prescribed documents, and **deliver a copy to
  the Registrar**. **381(2)** — the prescribed documents relating to the
  company's **latest consolidated financial statements** and such other
  statements as prescribed accompany them. **381(3)** — a **certified
  translation in English** is required for any document not in English.
  **381(4)** — the company shall send with those documents a **list, in the
  prescribed form, of all the places of business established by it in India as
  at the date of the balance sheet**.
- **Rules-level positions relied on:** the financial statements are prepared, as
  far as may be, in respect of the **Indian business operations** in accordance
  with **Schedule III** as applicable; the accounts of the Indian business
  operations must be **audited by a practising chartered accountant in India, or
  by a firm or LLP of practising chartered accountants**; and the statements are
  filed in **Form FC-3** within **six months of the close of the financial year**,
  the Registrar being empowered, for special reasons and on application, to
  extend that period by up to **three months**.

## Sections 387, 388 and 389, Companies Act 2013 — prospectus of an overseas company

- **Positions relied on (paraphrase):** **s. 387** — no person shall issue,
  circulate or distribute in India any **prospectus offering to subscribe for
  securities** of a company incorporated outside India unless the prospectus is
  **dated and signed** and contains particulars of **(a)** the instrument
  constituting or defining the constitution of the company; **(b)** the
  enactments, or provisions having the force of an enactment, under which the
  company was incorporated; **(c)** an **address in India** where the instrument,
  enactments or provisions, or copies thereof, can be **inspected** (with a
  certified translation where not in English); **(d)** the **date on which and the
  country in which** the company was incorporated; and **(e)** whether the company
  has **established a place of business in India** and, if so, the address of its
  **principal office in India** — and sets out the prescribed matters and reports.
  Exceptions: a prospectus issued to **existing members or debenture-holders**,
  whether or not the applicant has a right to renounce; and a prospectus relating
  to securities **uniform with securities previously issued and for the time being
  dealt in or quoted on a recognised stock exchange**. **s. 388** — no prospectus
  containing a statement purporting to be made by an **expert** may be issued in
  India unless the expert has **given and has not withdrawn his written consent**
  to the issue with the statement in the **form and context in which it appears**,
  and a **statement to that effect appears in the prospectus**; and unless the
  prospectus states on its face that a copy has been **delivered for registration**
  under s. 389; together with the particulars as to **application and allotment**.
  **s. 389** — before the prospectus is **issued, circulated or distributed** in
  India, a copy **certified by the chairperson of the company and two other
  directors** as having been approved by resolution of the **managing body** must
  be **delivered to the Registrar for registration**, the prospectus must **state
  on its face** that a copy has been so delivered, and the prescribed documents —
  **expert consents** and **material contracts** among them — must be endorsed on
  or attached to the copy.
- **Scope point relied on:** these sections bite on **any person** issuing in
  India, and apply **whether or not** the overseas company has established, or
  when formed will establish, a **place of business in India** — so they are not
  confined to "foreign companies" as defined in s. 2(42). This is why s. 379(1)
  lists only ss. 380 to 386 and ss. 392 and 393.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, ss. 387, 388 and 389 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026,
  not re-fetched. **Reviewer note:** please verify the **exact allocation** of
  content between ss. 387 and 388 (dating and particulars vs expert's consent and
  allotment), since q-i2c11-034, q-i2c11-035 and cs-i2c11-02-b each name a
  specific section.
- **Used in:** q-i2c11-008 (scope point), q-i2c11-034, q-i2c11-035, q-i2c11-036,
  cs-i2c11-02 (subs a and b), notes §8, common-mistakes items 13–14, one-page
  revision summary.

## Section 392, Companies Act 2013 — punishment for contravention

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** without prejudice to s. 391, if a foreign
  company contravenes the provisions of this Chapter, the **foreign company** is
  punishable with a **fine of not less than ₹1,00,000 which may extend to
  ₹3,00,000**, and in the case of a **continuing offence** with an **additional
  fine which may extend to ₹50,000 for every day** after the first during which
  the contravention continues; and **every officer of the foreign company who is
  in default** is punishable with a **fine of not less than ₹25,000 which may

## Sections 393 and 393A, Companies Act 2013 — validity of contracts and exemptions

- **Positions relied on (paraphrase):** **s. 393** — any failure by a company to
  comply with the provisions of this Chapter **shall not affect the validity of
  any contract, dealing or transaction** entered into by the company **or its
  liability to be sued** in respect thereof; **but** the company **shall not be
  entitled to bring any suit, claim any set-off, make any counter-claim or
  institute any legal proceeding** in respect of any such contract, dealing or
  transaction **until it has complied** with the provisions of this Act applicable
  to it. **s. 393A** — the **Central Government may, by notification, exempt any
  class or classes of foreign companies** from any of the provisions of **ss. 380
  to 386** and **ss. 392 and 393**, and a copy of every such notification shall,
  as soon as may be after it is made, be **laid before both Houses of
  Parliament**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, ss. 393 and 393A — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — flag for the reviewer:** **s. 393A was inserted by the
  Companies (Amendment) Act 2017.** Two features are load-bearing for the bank:
  the **exact list of sections the power reaches** (ss. 380 to 386 and ss. 392 and
  393 — **not** s. 379 and **not** ss. 387 to 391), on which **cs-i2c11-02-d**
  turns and which appears as common-mistakes item 15; and the fact that the power
  operates **by notification**, so the applicable law can move without any
  amendment to the Act. The chapter deliberately names **no notified class** — see
  the AMENDMENT-CHECK callout in notes §10. For **s. 393**, both halves are
  examined in **q-i2c11-038** (validity preserved; the company disabled from
