## Section 2(31), Companies Act 2013 — meaning of "deposit"

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** "deposit" **includes** any receipt of
  money by way of **deposit or loan or in any other form** by a company, but
  **does not include such categories of amount as may be prescribed** in
  consultation with the Reserve Bank of India. The three analytical points the
  notes draw from this (the definition is inclusive so the label is irrelevant;
  the exclusions are subordinate legislation and therefore volatile; a deposit
  is money the company must give back) are the SM's standard analysis, restated
  in our own words.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 2(31) — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not

## Section 73, Companies Act 2013 — prohibition and deposits from members

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **73(1)** — no company shall invite,
  accept or renew deposits from the **public** except in a manner provided under
  Chapter V, the proviso excepting a **banking company**, a **non-banking
  financial company** as defined in the RBI Act and registered with the RBI, and
  **such other company as the Central Government may specify after consultation
  with the RBI**. **73(2)** — a company may accept deposits from its **members**
  on terms agreed between them, including the provision of security if any,
  subject to a **resolution passed in general meeting** and to the prescribed
  rules, and subject to: **(a)** a **circular to members** showing the financial
  position of the company, the **credit rating obtained**, the total number of
  depositors and the amount due on previous deposits; **(b)** filing a copy of
  the circular with the statement with the Registrar **within thirty days before
  the date of issue**; **(c)** depositing, **on or before the thirtieth day of
  April each year**, a sum **not less than twenty per cent of the amount of its
  deposits maturing during the following financial year**, kept in a **scheduled
  bank** in a separate **deposit repayment reserve account**; **(d)** *(deposit
  insurance — omitted)*; **(e)** **certifying no default** in repayment of
  deposits accepted before or after the commencement of the Act or of interest,
  and where a default occurred, that it was **made good and five years have
  lapsed** since; **(f)** providing **security, if any**, including creation of a
  charge, with the Explanation that deposits not secured or only partly secured
  are **"unsecured deposits"**. **73(3)** — repayment with interest per the
  agreement. **73(4)** — on failure, the **depositor may apply to the Tribunal**
  for an order directing payment of the sum due, or for loss or damage incurred,
  and for such other orders as the Tribunal deems fit. **73(5)** — the deposit
  repayment reserve account **shall not be used for any purpose other than
  repayment of deposits**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 73 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — flag for the reviewer:** **clause (d) of s. 73(2)**
  (deposit insurance) is stated throughout this chapter as **OMITTED**, and it is
  used as the deliberate distractor in **q-i2c5-015**. The corresponding
  **rule 5** of the Deposit Rules (contract of insurance at least 30 days before
  the circular; minimum cover of ₹20,000 per depositor) is described in the notes
  as having **no operative parent**, and its figures are **not examined**. If the
  clause has been revived or replaced, **q-i2c5-015 and the §3 AMENDMENT-CHECK
  callout must both be reworked**. Section **73(5)** was inserted by amendment.
- **Used in:** q-i2c5-014, q-i2c5-015, q-i2c5-016, q-i2c5-017 (**numerical**),
  q-i2c5-018, q-i2c5-019, q-i2c5-020, cs-i2c5-03 (subs b (**numerical**) and c),

## MCA exemption notification for private companies (5 June 2015, as amended 13 June 2017)

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** clauses **(a) to (e) of s. 73(2)** do not
  apply to a private company **(A)** which accepts from its members monies **not
  exceeding one hundred per cent of the aggregate of its paid-up share capital,
  free reserves and securities premium account**; or **(B)** which is a
  **start-up**, for **ten years from the date of its incorporation**; or **(C)**
  which fulfils **all** of the following — it is **not an associate or a
  subsidiary** of any other company; its **borrowings from banks, financial
  institutions or any body corporate are less than twice its paid-up share
  capital or ₹50 crore, whichever is less**; and it has **not defaulted** in
  repaying such borrowings subsisting at the time of accepting deposits. The
  proviso requires the company to **file the details of monies so accepted with
  the Registrar** in the prescribed manner.
- **Source:** MCA notification dated 5 June 2015 as amended by the notification
  dated 13 June 2017, read with the proviso to rule 3(2) of the Deposit Rules —
  transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not re-fetched.

## Section 76 and rule 2(1)(e) — eligible companies and deposits from the public

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **76(1)** — notwithstanding s. 73, a
  **public company** having such **net worth or turnover as may be prescribed**
  may accept deposits from **persons other than its members**, subject to
  compliance with the requirements of **s. 73(2)** and to the prescribed rules;
  the **first proviso** requires a **rating** (covering net worth, liquidity and
  ability to pay deposits on the due date) from a **recognised credit rating
  agency** for informing the public **at the time of invitation and on every
  renewal**, the rating to be **obtained every year during the tenure of the
  deposits**, with a copy sent to the Registrar **along with the return of
  deposits**; the **second proviso** requires a company accepting **secured**
  deposits from the public to **create a charge on its assets within thirty days
  of acceptance**, of an amount not less than the deposits accepted, in favour of
  the deposit holders. **76(2)** — the rest of Chapter V applies **mutatis
  mutandis**. **Rule 2(1)(e)** — an "eligible company" is a **public company**
  having a **net worth of not less than ₹100 crore or a turnover of not less than
  ₹500 crore**, which has obtained the **prior consent of the company in general
  meeting by a special resolution** and **filed the resolution with the Registrar
  before making any invitation to the public**, with a proviso allowing an
  **ordinary resolution** where the deposits are **within the limits specified in
  s. 180(1)(c)**. **Rules 6 and 7** — the charge is on tangible assets valued by a
  registered valuer, created in favour of a **deposit trustee** whose written
  consent is obtained before the circular is issued, under a **deposit trust deed
  in Form DPT-2 executed at least seven days before** the circular or
  advertisement.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 76; Companies (Acceptance of Deposits)
  Rules 2014, rules 2(1)(e), 6 and 7 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not re-fetched.

  resolution proviso is examined in **cs-i2c5-02-b** (as a distractor). The
  **third proviso to s. 76(1)** (deposit insurance) shares the fate of

## Rule 3, Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014 — terms, tenure and ceilings

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **3(1)** — no company under s. 73(2) and
  no eligible company under s. 76 shall accept or renew a deposit **repayable on
  demand or on notice within a period of less than six months or more than
  thirty-six months** from acceptance or renewal; the **proviso** allows deposits
  repayable earlier than six months **for short-term requirements of funds**,
  provided they do **not exceed ten per cent of the aggregate of paid-up share
  capital, free reserves and securities premium account** and are **not repayable
  earlier than three months**. **3(2)** — members' deposits of a company
  accepting under s. 73(2) may not, together with deposits outstanding on the
  date of acceptance or renewal, exceed **thirty-five per cent** of that
  aggregate, with a **proviso** for the private-company relaxation and the DPT-3
  filing. **3(3)** — an eligible company may not accept or renew deposits **from
  its members** exceeding **ten per cent**, nor **any other deposit** exceeding
  **twenty-five per cent**, of that aggregate. **3(4)** — a **Government company**
  eligible under s. 76 is capped at **thirty-five per cent** in the aggregate.
  **3(5)** — no deposit repayable on demand. **3(6)** — no deposit carrying a
  **rate of interest**, and no **brokerage**, exceeding the maximum rate or
  brokerage **prescribed by the RBI for acceptance of deposits by non-banking
  financial companies**, with an Explanation defining brokerage to include any
  commission by whatever name paid to a person authorised to solicit deposits but
  to exclude legal, rating and advertisement expenses. **3(7)** — the company may
  **not reserve to itself, directly or indirectly, any right to alter the terms**
  of the deposit, the trust deed or the insurance contract **to the prejudice or
  disadvantage of the depositor** after the circular is issued and deposits
  accepted. The Rules also permit deposits **in joint names not exceeding three**,
  with or without clauses such as "Jointly", "Either or Survivor", "First named or
  Survivor" and "Anyone or Survivor".
- **Source:** Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules 2014, rule 3 —
  transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not re-fetched. **Reviewer note:** the notes cite the
  **joint-names cap** to "the Deposit Rules" without a sub-rule number, because
  its location has moved between amendments; please supply the correct sub-rule
  when checking.

  25% to 35%** by an amendment; the private-company proviso was rewritten when

## Rules 4, 5, 10, 11, 14, 16 and 16A — circulars, records and the annual return

- **Positions relied on (paraphrase):** **rule 4** — the circular to members and
  the circular in the form of an advertisement to the public are issued in **Form
  DPT-1**, delivered to the Registrar **not less than thirty days before the date
  of issue**, signed by a **majority of the directors** as constituted when the
  Board approved it; the circular or advertisement is **valid until the expiry of
  six months from the end of the financial year in which it is issued or until
  the date on which the financial statement is laid before the company in the
  annual general meeting (or, where no such meeting was held, the latest day on
  which it should have been held), whichever is earlier**, and a **fresh circular
  must be issued in each financial year**. **Rule 5** — deposit insurance;
  described in the notes as having **no operative parent** following the omission
  of s. 73(2)(d), and **not examined**. **Rule 10** — the application form for a
  deposit contains a declaration by the applicant that the deposit is not being
  made out of money borrowed from another person. **Rule 11** — a **deposit
  receipt** is furnished **within twenty-one days** of receipt of the money or
  realisation of the cheque. **Rule 14** — a **register of deposits** is kept at
  the **registered office** with the prescribed particulars, entries being made
  **within seven days** of issuing the deposit receipt and **authenticated by a
  director, the company secretary or an officer authorised by the Board**, the
  register being **preserved in good order for not less than eight years** from
  the financial year in which the latest entry is made. **Rule 16** — a **return
  of deposits in Form DPT-3** is filed with the Registrar **on or before the
  thirtieth day of June of every year**, furnishing information **as on the
  thirty-first day of March** of that year, **duly audited by the company's
  auditor**. **Rule 16A** — disclosure in the financial statements of money
  received from directors (and, for a private company, from directors **and their
  relatives**), and reporting through DPT-3 of **outstanding receipts of money or
  loans not treated as deposits**.

## Rules 15 and 17 — premature repayment and penal interest

- **Positions relied on (paraphrase):** **rule 15** — where a company repays a
  deposit at the depositor's request **after the expiry of six months from the
  date of the deposit but before the expiry of the period for which it was
  accepted**, the **rate of interest payable is reduced by one per cent from the
  rate the company would have paid had the deposit been accepted for the period
  for which it has run**; the reduction does **not** apply where the depositor is
  instead **permitted to renew** the deposit before its expiry. **Rule 17** — a
  company must pay **penal interest at eighteen per cent per annum for the
  overdue period** on a deposit, whether secured or unsecured, that has
  **matured and been claimed but remains unpaid**.

## Sections 74 and 75, Companies Act 2013 — pre-commencement deposits and fraud

- **Positions relied on (paraphrase):** **74(1)** — where a deposit accepted
  **before the commencement of the Act**, or interest on it, remains unpaid on
  commencement or becomes due thereafter, the company shall **(a)** file with the
  Registrar **within three months** of the commencement or of the date the
  payments became due a **statement of all such deposits and the sums remaining
  unpaid with interest, together with the arrangements made for repayment**
  (Form **DPT-4**), and **(b)** **repay within three years** of the commencement
  or **on or before the expiry of the period for which the deposits were
  accepted, whichever is earlier**; a proviso requires any **renewal** to comply
  with Chapter V and the Rules. **74(2)** — the **Tribunal** may, on the
  company's application and after considering its financial condition, the amount
  of the deposit and the interest payable and such other matters, **allow further
  time** as it considers reasonable. **74(3)** — failure to repay within the time
  specified or the further time allowed makes the company punishable **in
  addition to the payment of the deposit and interest due**. **75(1)** — where
  the company so fails **and it is proved that the deposits had been accepted
  with intent to defraud the depositors or for any fraudulent purpose**, every
  **officer responsible for the acceptance** of such deposit is, **without
  prejudice to s. 74(3) and liability under s. 447**, **personally responsible,
  without any limitation of liability**, for all or any of the losses or damages
  incurred by the depositors. **75(2)** — the suit, proceeding or other action may
  be taken by **any person, group of persons or association of persons** who
  incurred loss.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, ss. 74 and 75 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — flag for the reviewer:** **s. 74(1)(b)** read **"within
  one year from such commencement or from the date on which such payments are
  due, whichever is earlier"** as enacted and was **substituted** by an amendment
  Act with the **three-year / original-tenure** formulation used here.
  **q-i2c5-037 turns entirely on this**, with the one-year period as the named
  pre-amendment distractor. **Section 74(3)** is stated here as routing the
  company's punishment through **s. 76A** rather than carrying its own rupee
  figures; the earlier text prescribed a fine of not less than ₹1 crore extending

## Section 76A, Companies Act 2013 — punishment for contravention

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** where a company **accepts or invites, or
  allows or causes any other person to accept or invite on its behalf, any
  deposit in contravention of the manner or the conditions** prescribed under
  s. 73 or s. 76 or the rules made thereunder, **or fails to repay** the deposit
  or interest within the time specified or such further time as the Tribunal may
  allow, then **(a)** the **company** shall, **in addition to the payment of the
  amount of the deposit or part thereof and the interest due**, be liable to a
  **penalty of ₹1 crore or twice the amount of deposits accepted by the company,
  whichever is lower, which may extend to ₹10 crore**; and **(b)** **every officer
  of the company who is in default** shall be liable to **imprisonment which may
  extend to seven years and to a fine** within the prescribed band; with a
  **proviso** that an officer who has contravened the provisions **knowingly or
  wilfully with the intention to deceive** the company, its shareholders,
  depositors, creditors or the tax authorities is liable for action under
  **s. 447**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 76A (inserted 2015, amended subsequently) —
  transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — flag for the reviewer:** the company's liability was
  converted from a **fine** to a **penalty**; the officer's punishment has moved
  between **"imprisonment or fine"** and **"imprisonment and fine"**, and the
  **rupee band for the officer's fine has been revised**. These notes therefore
  state the officer's fine **qualitatively** ("a fine within the prescribed
  band") and **no question examines it** — the AMENDMENT-CHECK callout in §10
  says so expressly. What **is** examined is the company-side arithmetic:
  **cs-i2c5-02-d (numerical)** computes the **lower** of ₹1 crore and twice the
  deposits accepted, and the verifier function `cs_i2c5_02_d` recomputes it with
  `min()` rather than hard-coding a figure. If the "whichever is lower"
  formulation, the ₹1 crore anchor or the ₹10 crore ceiling has changed, that
  question, its options and the verifier must be reworked together.
- **Used in:** cs-i2c5-02 (sub d, **numerical**), d-i2c5-05 (point 4), notes §10
  and Scenario 6, common-mistakes item 10, one-page revision summary.
