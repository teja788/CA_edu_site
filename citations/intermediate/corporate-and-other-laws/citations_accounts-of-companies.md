## Section 128, Companies Act 2013 — books of account

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** every company shall prepare and keep at
  its registered office books of account, other relevant books and papers and
  the financial statement for every financial year, giving a true and fair view
  of the state of affairs of the company including that of its branch offices,
  and explaining the transactions effected both at the registered office and at
  the branches, kept **on accrual basis and according to the double entry
  system** (128(1)). **First proviso:** all or any of them may be kept at such
  other place **in India** as the **Board may decide**, and on such a decision
  the company shall **within seven days thereof** file with the **Registrar** a
  **notice in writing giving the full address** of that other place. **Second
  proviso:** the books may be kept **in electronic mode** in the prescribed
  manner. **128(2)** — a branch office in or outside India is covered where
  **proper books relating to its transactions are kept at that office** and
  **proper summarised returns are sent periodically** to the registered office
  or the other place. **128(3)–(4)** — inspection at the registered office or
  the other place in India **by any director during business hours**; **copies**
  of financial information maintained outside the country to be maintained and
  produced on the prescribed conditions; officers and employees to give all
  reasonable assistance. **128(5)** — books of a period of **not less than
  eight financial years immediately preceding a financial year**, with the
  **relevant vouchers**, to be kept in good order (all preceding years where the
  company is younger); proviso — where an **investigation has been ordered under
  Chapter XIV** the **Central Government may direct** a longer period.
  **128(6)** — the **managing director, the whole-time director in charge of
  finance, the Chief Financial Officer or any other person charged by the
  Board** is liable to a **penalty of ₹50,000**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 128 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **Rules-level and amendment-sensitive (NOT examined in the bank):** the
  electronic-mode conditions in **Rule 3, Companies (Accounts) Rules 2014** —
  accessibility in India, retention in the original format, the
  **daily back-up on servers physically located in India**, the **annual
  intimation** to the Registrar of the service provider, its IP address and the
  location of the server, and the **audit-trail (edit-log)** requirement for
  accounting software, which was inserted and then deferred more than once. The
  notes state these inside an AMENDMENT-CHECK callout and attribute them to the
  Rules, never to the section. Rule 4 (inspection of a **subsidiary's** books
  only by a person **authorised by a Board resolution**) is mentioned in a
  mistake callout and is likewise not examined.

  eight-financial-year window), q-i2c9-005, q-i2c9-006 (**numerical** —
  seven-day notice), cs-i2c9-03 (sub a), d-i2c9-01 (all points), notes §1 and

## Section 129, Companies Act 2013 — financial statements and consolidation

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **129(1)** — the financial statements
  shall give a **true and fair view** of the state of affairs, **comply with the
  accounting standards notified under s. 133**, and be in the **form or forms
  provided for different classes of companies in Schedule III**; the proviso
  disapplies the Schedule III form to **insurance companies, banking companies
  and companies engaged in the generation or supply of electricity** and any
  other class governed by a special Act, to the extent that Act provides.
  **129(2)** — the Board **lays** the financial statements at **every annual
  general meeting**. **129(3)** — where a company has **one or more subsidiaries
  or associate companies** it shall, **in addition to its own financial
  statements**, prepare a **consolidated financial statement** in the **same
  form and manner** and lay it before the annual general meeting; the proviso
  requires a **statement containing the salient features** of the financial
  statement of each such entity in the prescribed form; the **Explanation**
  provides that **"subsidiary" includes an associate company and a joint
  venture**. **129(4)** — the provisions apply **mutatis mutandis** to the
  consolidated financial statement. **129(5)** — where the statements do not
  comply with the accounting standards, the company shall disclose the
  **deviation**, the **reasons** and the **financial effects, if any**.
  **129(6)** — the **Central Government** may exempt a class of companies from
  any requirement of the section, on conditions, in the public interest.
  **129(7)** — the **managing director, the whole-time director in charge of
  finance, the Chief Financial Officer, any other person charged by the Board
  and, in the absence of any of them, all the directors** are liable.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 129 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE / rules-level — flag for the reviewer:** the
  **exemptions from preparing consolidated financial statements** (the
  intermediate wholly-owned subsidiary whose ultimate or intermediate holding
  company files compliant CFS and whose members do not object; the first-year
  carve-out for a company having only associates or joint ventures; the
  unlisted-company transitional carve-outs) sit in **Rule 6, Companies
  (Accounts) Rules 2014** and have been amended more than once. The notes flag
  this in an AMENDMENT-CHECK callout and **no question turns on any exemption**.
  **Form AOC-1** is named in q-i2c9-010, with **AOC-2, AOC-4 and MGT-9** as the
  named distractors; if the form numbering has changed, that question and its
  explanations must be reworked.
- **Used in:** q-i2c9-007, q-i2c9-008, q-i2c9-009, q-i2c9-010, q-i2c9-011,
  d-i2c9-02 (all points), notes §2, common-mistakes item 9, one-page revision
  summary.

## Section 129A, Companies Act 2013 — periodical financial results

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** the **Central Government** may, **in

## Section 130, Companies Act 2013 — re-opening of accounts on a court's or the Tribunal's order

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** a company **shall not re-open its books
  of account and shall not re-cast its financial statements** unless an
  application is made by the **Central Government**, the **income-tax
  authorities**, the **Securities and Exchange Board**, **any other statutory
  regulatory body or authority** or **any person concerned**, and an **order is
  made by a court of competent jurisdiction or the Tribunal** to the effect that
  the relevant **earlier accounts were prepared in a fraudulent manner**, or that
  the **affairs of the company were mismanaged** during the relevant period
  casting a doubt on the reliability of the financial statements. The proviso
  requires **notice** to the Central Government, the income-tax authorities,
  SEBI or any other statutory regulatory body concerned and **any other person
  concerned**, and **consideration of their representations**. **130(2)** — the
  accounts so revised or re-cast are **final**. **130(3)** — **no order** for
  re-opening books relating to a period **earlier than eight financial years
  immediately preceding the current financial year**, save that where a
  direction has been issued under **s. 128(5)** for a longer preservation period,
  the books may be ordered re-opened **within that longer period**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 130 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched. **Reviewer note:** s. 130(3) was **inserted by the Companies
  (Amendment) Act 2017**; confirm the eight-financial-year figure and the
  s. 128(5) cross-reference, because **cs-i2c9-03-b is a verified numerical that
  turns entirely on it** and q-i2c9-004 uses the parallel s. 128(5) window.
- **Used in:** q-i2c9-013, q-i2c9-014, cs-i2c9-03 (sub b, **numerical**),
  d-i2c9-03 (points 1–3), notes §3 and Scenario 2, common-mistakes items 3 and

## Section 131, Companies Act 2013 — voluntary revision of financial statements or the Board's report

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** if it appears to the **directors** that
  the **financial statement** or the **report of the Board** does not comply with
  **s. 129** or **s. 134**, they **may** prepare a **revised financial statement
  or a revised report** in respect of **any of the three preceding financial
  years**, after obtaining the **approval of the Tribunal** on an application
  made by the company in the prescribed form and manner; the Tribunal gives
  **notice to the Central Government and the income-tax authorities** and takes
  their representations into consideration. **Proviso:** such revised statement
  or report **shall not be prepared or filed more than once in a financial
  year**, and the **detailed reasons for revision** shall be **disclosed in the
  Board's report** of the relevant financial year in which the revision is made.
  **131(2)** — where copies of the previous documents have been **sent out to
  members**, **delivered to the Registrar** or **laid before the company in
  general meeting**, the revisions must be **confined** to the correction for the
  non-compliance and to **necessary consequential alterations**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 131 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — flag for the reviewer:** the two figures that carry
  the marks are the **three preceding financial years** and the **once in a
  financial year** limit, and the approving authority is the **Tribunal alone**
  (there is no "court of competent jurisdiction" limb here, unlike s. 130).
  **q-i2c9-015, q-i2c9-016 and cs-i2c9-03-c turn on these**, with the
  eight-financial-year period and the Central Government named as the
  distractors. Verify before initialling.
- **Used in:** q-i2c9-015, q-i2c9-016, cs-i2c9-03 (sub c), d-i2c9-03 (points
  4–5), notes §3 and Scenario 2, common-mistakes items 3 and 4, one-page

## Section 132, Companies Act 2013 — National Financial Reporting Authority

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** the **Central Government** may by
  notification constitute NFRA for matters relating to **accounting and auditing
  standards**; it consists of a **chairperson**, being a person of eminence with
  expertise in **accountancy, auditing, finance or law**, appointed by the
  Central Government, and **such other members not exceeding fifteen**.
  **Functions:** recommend to the Central Government the formulation and laying
  down of accounting and auditing policies and standards; **monitor and enforce
  compliance**; **oversee the quality of service** of the professions associated
  with ensuring compliance and suggest measures for improvement; and perform such
  other functions as may be prescribed. **Powers (132(4)):** **investigate**,
  suo motu or on a **reference by the Central Government**, matters of
  **professional or other misconduct** committed by a **member or firm of
  chartered accountants** registered under the **Chartered Accountants Act
  1949**; **no other institute or body** may initiate or continue proceedings in
  such a matter once NFRA has done so; the **same powers as a civil court** for
  discovery, production of documents, summoning and examining on oath and
  inspection of books and records; and, where misconduct is **proved**, a
  **monetary penalty** and **debarment** from appointment as an **auditor or
  internal auditor**, or from undertaking any audit, for a **minimum of six
  months and a maximum of ten years**. An **appeal** lies to the **Appellate
  Tribunal**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 132 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — flag for the reviewer:** the **monetary penalty slabs**
  in s. 132(4)(B) (a floor for individuals and a higher floor for firms, each
  capped at a multiple of the fees received), the identity of the **appellate
  forum**, and the classes of companies covered by the **NFRA Rules 2018** have
  all been touched by amendment. The notes therefore state the penalty
  **qualitatively** and state only the **6-month-to-10-year debarment**.
  **No bank question turns on an NFRA rupee figure**; q-i2c9-018 turns only on
  the boundary between NFRA's recommending role and the Central Government's
  prescribing power under s. 133.
- **Used in:** q-i2c9-017, q-i2c9-018, notes §4, one-page revision summary.

## Section 134, Companies Act 2013 — signing of accounts and the Board's report

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **134(1)** — the financial statements,
  **including the consolidated financial statement if any**, shall be **approved
  by the Board before they are signed** on behalf of the Board **at least by**
  the **chairperson of the company where he is authorised by the Board**, **or
  by two directors of whom one shall be the managing director**, **and** the
  **Chief Executive Officer if he is a director**, the **Chief Financial
  Officer** and the **company secretary**, **wherever they are appointed**; for
  a **One Person Company**, by **only one director**. **134(2)** — the
  **auditor's report** shall be **attached to every financial statement**.
  **134(3)** — the contents of the Board's report, including the **web address
  where the annual return referred to in s. 92(3) has been placed**; the
  **number of meetings of the Board**; the **Directors' Responsibility
  Statement**; details of **frauds reported by auditors under s. 143(12)** other
  than those reportable to the Central Government; the **declaration by
  independent directors** under s. 149(6); the **remuneration policy** for
  companies covered by s. 178(1); **explanations or comments by the Board** on
  every qualification, reservation, adverse remark or disclaimer in the
  **auditor's report** and the **secretarial audit report**; particulars of
  **loans, guarantees or investments** under **s. 186**; particulars of
  **contracts or arrangements with related parties** under **s. 188(1)**; the
  **state of the company's affairs**; amounts **carried to reserves**; the
  **dividend recommended**; **material changes and commitments** affecting the
  financial position between the end of the financial year and the date of the
  report; **conservation of energy, technology absorption and foreign exchange
  earnings and outgo**; the **risk management policy**; the **development and
  implementation of the CSR policy**; and, for listed and prescribed public
  companies, the **formal annual evaluation** of the Board's performance.
  **134(5)** — the six statements of the **Directors' Responsibility
  Statement**. **134(6)** — the report is signed by the **chairperson if
  authorised by the Board**, else by **at least two directors one of whom shall
  be a managing director**, or by **the director where there is only one
  director**. **134(8)** — penalty of **₹3,00,000** on the company and
  **₹50,000** on every officer in default.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 134 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — the single most likely stale point in this chapter:**
  **s. 134(3)(a)** originally required an **extract of the annual return** in
  **Form MGT-9** to be annexed to the Board's report; it was **substituted** so

  extract of the annual return in Form MGT-9" as the named pre-amendment
  distractor. Also amendment-sensitive: **s. 134(3A)** (abridged Board's report
  for a **One Person Company and a small company**, rules-level, mentioned in an
  AMENDMENT-CHECK callout and **not examined**), and the conversion of the
  s. 134(8) **fine into a penalty**. Verify before initialling.
- **Used in:** q-i2c9-020, q-i2c9-021, q-i2c9-022, q-i2c9-023, q-i2c9-024,
  cs-i2c9-03 (sub c, as the non-compliance triggering s. 131), d-i2c9-04 (all

## Section 135, Companies Act 2013 — corporate social responsibility

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **135(1)** — every company having,
  **during the immediately preceding financial year**, a **net worth of ₹500
  crore or more**, **or** a **turnover of ₹1,000 crore or more**, **or** a **net
  profit of ₹5 crore or more** shall constitute a **CSR Committee of the Board**
  of **three or more directors, at least one of whom shall be an independent
  director**; **first proviso** — a company **not required to appoint an
  independent director under s. 149(4)** shall have **two or more directors** on
  the committee. **135(2)** — the Board's report discloses the **composition**.
  **135(3)** — the committee **formulates and recommends the CSR Policy**
  indicating the activities specified in **Schedule VII**, **recommends the
  amount**, and **monitors** the policy. **135(4)** — the Board **approves the
  policy**, **discloses its contents in its report**, **places it on the
  website**, and **ensures the activities are undertaken**. **135(5)** — the
  Board shall ensure the company spends, **in every financial year**, **at least
  two per cent of the average net profits of the three immediately preceding
  financial years**, with the average taken over the completed years where fewer
  than three have elapsed; **first proviso** — preference to the **local area**;
  **second proviso** — where the company fails to spend, the Board shall state
  the reasons in its report under **s. 134(3)(o)** and, **unless the unspent
  amount relates to an ongoing project**, **transfer it to a Fund specified in
  Schedule VII within six months of the expiry of the financial year**; **third
  proviso** — an **excess spend may be set off** against the requirement for up
  to the **three immediately succeeding financial years**, subject to prescribed
  conditions; **Explanation** — **"net profit" is calculated in accordance with
  s. 198** and excludes such sums as may be prescribed. **135(6)** — an unspent
  amount **pursuant to an ongoing project** shall be transferred **within thirty
  days from the end of the financial year** to a special account in any
  **scheduled bank** called the **Unspent Corporate Social Responsibility
  Account** for that financial year, spent **within three financial years from
  the date of transfer**, failing which it goes to a **Fund specified in
  Schedule VII within thirty days from the completion of the third financial
  year**. **135(7)** — the **company** is liable to a penalty of **twice the
  amount required to be transferred** or **₹1 crore, whichever is less**, and
  **every officer in default** to **one-tenth of that amount** or **₹2 lakh,
  whichever is less**. **135(9)** — where the amount to be spent **does not
  exceed ₹50 lakh**, **no CSR Committee** is required and the **Board discharges
  its functions**.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 135 — transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not
  re-fetched. Sub-sections (6) to (9) and the second and third provisos to
  sub-section (5) were inserted or substituted by the **Companies (Amendment)
  Acts 2019 and 2020**.
- **AMENDMENT-SENSITIVE — the most heavily examined and most volatile section
  in the chapter.** **Ten bank items turn on its figures**: the applicability
  thresholds (q-i2c9-025, q-i2c9-026 **numerical**), the 2% of a three-year
  average (q-i2c9-027 **numerical**, cs-i2c9-01-a **numerical**), the committee

  excess set-off (cs-i2c9-01-d), and the penalty caps (q-i2c9-032
  **numerical**). If **any** of 500/1,000/5 crore, 2%, ₹50 lakh, 30 days,

- **Rules-level and deliberately NOT examined:** the **Companies (CSR Policy)
  Rules 2014** definition of **"net profit"** (excluding profits from overseas

## Section 136, Companies Act 2013 — right of a member to copies of the audited financial statements

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** a copy of the **financial statements**,
  including the **consolidated financial statements**, the **auditor's report**
  and **every other document required by law to be annexed or attached**, shall
  be **sent to every member**, to **every trustee for the debenture-holders** and
  to **all other persons entitled to receive notice of the general meeting**,
  **not less than twenty-one days before the date of the meeting**. **First
  proviso:** where they are sent **less than twenty-one days** before, they are
  **deemed duly sent** if **so agreed** by members holding — where the company
  has a **share capital** — a **majority in number entitled to vote** who
  **represent not less than ninety-five per cent of such part of the paid-up
  share capital as gives a right to vote**; and, where the company has **no
  share capital**, by members having **not less than ninety-five per cent of the
  total voting power**. **Listed companies:** deemed compliance where the
  documents are kept for **inspection at the registered office during working
  hours for twenty-one days** before the meeting and a **statement containing the
  salient features** in the prescribed form, or copies of the documents, is sent
  twenty-one days before; the documents are also **placed on the website**, and a
  listed company **having a subsidiary** places the **separate audited accounts
  of each subsidiary** on its website and gives a copy to any shareholder who

## Section 137, Companies Act 2013 — copy of financial statements to be filed with the Registrar

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **137(1)** — a copy of the financial
  statements, **including the consolidated financial statement**, along with all
  the documents required to be attached, **duly adopted at the annual general
  meeting**, shall be **filed with the Registrar within thirty days of the date
  of the annual general meeting** with the prescribed fee. **First proviso** —
  where the financial statements are **not adopted** at the annual general
  meeting or at the **adjourned** annual general meeting, they shall be filed
  **within thirty days of the date of the annual general meeting** and the
  Registrar shall **take them in his records as provisional** until the adopted
  statements are filed **within thirty days of the date of the adjourned
  meeting**. **Further proviso** — a **One Person Company** shall file the
  financial statements **duly adopted by its member** within **one hundred and
  eighty days from the closure of the financial year**. **Further proviso** —
  the accounts of a **subsidiary incorporated outside India** that has **not
  established a place of business in India** shall be attached. **137(2)** —
  where the **annual general meeting has not been held**, the financial
  statements, the documents required to be attached and **the reasons for not
  holding the meeting** shall be filed **within thirty days of the last date
  before which the annual general meeting should have been held**. **137(3)** —
  the **company** is liable to a penalty of **₹10,000** and, for continuing
  failure, a further **₹100 per day** subject to **₹2,00,000**; the **managing
  director and the Chief Financial Officer** (or the **director charged by the
  Board**, and in the absence of any such director **all the directors**) to

## Section 138, Companies Act 2013 — internal audit

- **Position relied on (paraphrase):** **138(1)** — **such class or classes of
  companies as may be prescribed** shall be required to **appoint an internal
  auditor**, who shall be a **chartered accountant** or a **cost accountant**, or
  **such other professional as may be decided by the Board**, to conduct the
  internal audit of the functions and activities of the company. **138(2)** —
  the **Central Government** may prescribe the **manner and the intervals** in
  which the internal audit shall be conducted and reported to the Board.
- **Prescribed classes relied on (rules-level):** **every listed company**;
  **every unlisted public company** having, **during the preceding financial
  year**, **paid-up share capital of ₹50 crore or more**, **or turnover of ₹200
  crore or more**, **or outstanding loans or borrowings from banks or public
  financial institutions exceeding ₹100 crore at any point of time**, **or
  outstanding deposits of ₹25 crore or more at any point of time**; and **every
  private company** having, during the preceding financial year, **turnover of
  ₹200 crore or more**, **or outstanding loans or borrowings from banks or
  public financial institutions exceeding ₹100 crore at any point of time**. The
  internal auditor **may or may not be an employee** of the company, and a
  chartered accountant may be **engaged in practice or not**. The **Audit
  Committee, or the Board where there is no Audit Committee**, shall **in
  consultation with the internal auditor** formulate the **scope, functioning,
  periodicity and methodology** of the internal audit.
- **Source:** Companies Act 2013, s. 138, and **Rule 13 of the Companies
  (Accounts) Rules 2014** — both transcribed 27 Aug 2026, not re-fetched.
